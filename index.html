Welcome to Meals & Money

MealsNMoney helps you plan food and groceries that fit your diet preferences and budget.

Set your own targets, log food and receipts, and see your week at a glance.

MealsNMoney — Food and money in one app.

What MealsNMoney Does

📋 Track Food and Spending

  • Log what you eat across breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
  • Search a grocery catalog or the USDA nutrition reference, or photograph the plate
  • Record grocery spending by hand or from a receipt photo

🛒 Budget-First Grocery Lists

  • Set a weekly budget and get a grocery list of US staples, sorted by aisle
  • Prices shown as ranges, and the list is built to fit the top of the range
  • Your calorie target acts as a ceiling; a protein target gets spare budget

🧾 Receipts and Pantry

  • Photograph a receipt and check the total before it is saved
  • Extract and Save reads the lines into a virtual pantry, food and non-food
  • Anything the app cannot classify is flagged for you to check, not guessed

🛡️ Allergies and Preferences

  • Add allergies from the US Big 9 or type your own; the grocery list screens them out with a fixed rule check, no AI involved
  • Diet preferences such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten free and dairy free filter the list
  • Find food pantries near your ZIP code

🎯 Your Numbers

You type your own calorie target and weekly budget. The app suggests neither.

📊 Describe and Calculate

MealsNMoney records and adds up. It does not tell you what to eat and makes no health claims.

❓ Unknown Is Never Zero

Where a nutrition figure is not known, the app says so instead of showing 0.

MealsNMoney is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional advice. The allergy filter can only screen the allergen data it has. Always read the label.
