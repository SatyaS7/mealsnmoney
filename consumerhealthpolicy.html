MealsNMoney Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 02nd, 2026

Some state laws, including in Washington and Nevada, treat information about your health as consumer health data and give you separate rights over it. This document describes how we handle it.

The only consumer health data we collect is the food allergies you choose to record in your profile. We collect it only with your consent, which we ask for on the screen where you record it. We use it only to filter suggestions and to flag matching items in your log. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with our analytics provider, our subscription management provider, or any advertiser. We keep it until you remove it or delete your account.

We share it only with the cloud hosting and authentication providers named in Section 5 of our Privacy Policy, which process it on our behalf under a written contract that limits them to that purpose.

You may review, correct, or delete the allergies we hold at any time in the app, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. If you withdraw consent, we stop collecting and using the data, we delete what we hold, and we instruct our processors to do the same. To exercise a right under this document, or to ask us a question about it, email support@mealsnmoney.com. If we refuse, you may appeal as described in Section 8 of our Privacy Policy. If you are in Washington State you may also complain to the Washington Attorney General.
