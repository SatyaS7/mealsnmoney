Delete Your MealsNMoney Account

Last Updated: September 08th, 2026

MealsNMoney is published by Thirty Seven Labs Inc. This page explains how to delete your account and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open MealsNMoney and sign in.
  2. Tap the account menu in the top right.
  3. Select My profile.
  4. Choose Delete account.
  5. Read the confirmation message and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

If you cannot sign in

Email support@mealsnmoney.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We do this within 30 days.

What is deleted

We remove all of the following from our live systems immediately:

  • your profile, including your email address, ZIP code, diet preferences and food allergies
  • your food and nutrition entries, and any calories-burned entries
  • your calorie targets and weekly food budgets
  • your grocery lists
  • your spending records and the itemized lines from any receipt you scanned
  • and your sign-in account itself

We also instruct our analytics provider and our subscription management provider to delete the records they hold against your account identifier. We do this within 30 days of the deletion.

What is kept, and for how long

We keep only what we are required to keep for legal, security, or compliance obligations. That is a record of your acceptance of our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and of any arbitration opt-out you sent us. Those records include your email address and the name you entered, and are kept for six years after your account closes.

Copies of deleted data may remain in our routine encrypted backups for up to 35 days. They are not restored to live systems and are overwritten on the ordinary backup cycle.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or by Apple, and you cancel them in your Google Play or App Store account settings. We cannot cancel a subscription for you or issue a refund.
