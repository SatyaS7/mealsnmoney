MealsNMoney Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26th, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Thirty Seven Labs Inc. (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use MealsNMoney, including:

  • the MealsNMoney mobile applications
  • the MealsNMoney website
  • and related services (the “Service”).

By using the Service you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

MealsNMoney records what you eat and what you spend on food. We treat that as sensitive information and collect as little of it as the Service needs to work.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account we collect your email address. Authentication is handled by Amazon Cognito, a third-party identity service. Your password is set and verified by that service and is never stored on our own servers in any readable form.

2.2 Profile Information

You may provide a ZIP code, diet preferences, and food allergies. We use these to filter grocery and meal suggestions and to find food assistance resources near you.

We do not collect medical conditions or diagnoses.

The Service has no field for a medical condition, and it does not ask for one. Diet preferences are lifestyle and taste choices, such as vegetarian, vegan, dairy free, and gluten free. Food allergies are collected only so that matching items can be filtered out of suggestions and flagged in your log, as described in the Terms of Service. Depending on where you live, information about food allergies may be treated as health-related information under local law, and we handle it accordingly.

2.3 Food and Nutrition Entries

We store the food entries you create, including the food name, portion or weight, date, and the calorie and nutrient figures associated with the entry. We also store any calories-burned entries you record, along with the activity and duration they came from.

2.4 Goals and Budgets

We store the calorie targets and the weekly grocery budgets you set, the grocery lists generated for you, and your acceptance of any in-app acknowledgment, including the version shown and the date and time you accepted it.

2.5 Spending and Receipt Information

We store the food and grocery spending entries you record, including amount, date, and any note or store name you provide, and the itemized lines extracted from a receipt you choose to scan.

When a receipt is processed we deliberately discard payment card numbers, loyalty or membership numbers, and cashier or employee names. We do not want that information and do not retain it.

2.6 Photographs

Photographs are processed and then discarded. We do not store your meal photos or your receipt images.

When you photograph a meal or a receipt, the image is sent for processing, the relevant details are extracted and returned to you as a draft for review, and the image is discarded. It is not written to our databases and not written to file storage. Only the entries you review and confirm are saved to your account.

2.7 Device, Usage, and Analytics Information

We may collect limited technical information such as the app version and build, device and operating system type, IP address, and diagnostic and error logs. We use this to keep the Service running and to diagnose faults.

We also use PostHog, a third-party product analytics service, to understand which features of the Service are used and whether they are working. PostHog receives an account identifier, so what it holds is linked to your account rather than anonymous. It also receives your subscription tier, whether you have completed onboarding, how you signed up, your platform, and your app version.

Nothing derived from your food, your health, or your money is sent to our analytics provider.

Our analytics provider is never sent food names or brands, calorie or nutrient values, weight, budget or spending amounts, store names, ZIP codes, allergies or diet preferences, anything you typed into a search, or anything read off a receipt. Searches for food pantries and food assistance resources are not recorded in analytics at all, and neither is any result of the allergen filter.

2.8 Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device by your operating system. The reminder time is stored on your device. We do not need or use a push notification service to deliver it.

2.9 What We Do Not Collect

  • medical conditions, diagnoses, prescriptions, or clinical records
  • precise device location or GPS data, the Service uses only the ZIP code you type in
  • full payment card numbers, which are handled by the app store or payment provider and never reach our servers
  • contacts, photo library contents beyond the image you choose to submit, or microphone data
  • and information about anyone under 18, whom the Service is not for.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • authenticate you and manage your account
  • provide and maintain the Service
  • record and display the food, nutrition, and spending information you enter
  • generate grocery lists that fit the budget you set
  • filter suggestions according to the diet preferences and food allergies you record
  • find food pantries and food assistance resources near the ZIP code you provide
  • send you the daily reminder if you have enabled it
  • identify bugs, errors, abuse, or misuse of the Service
  • and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your personal information to make decisions about your health, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Artificial Intelligence Features and Third-Party Processing

Some features of the Service use a third-party artificial intelligence provider, currently OpenAI, to read a photograph of a meal or of a receipt and propose entries, and to search publicly available sources for food assistance resources near a ZIP code.

Where such a feature is used, the image or the query is transmitted to that provider for processing and the result is returned to you as a draft. We access these providers through their business application programming interfaces under a data processing agreement. Content submitted through those interfaces is not used to train the provider models by default.

AI-generated output is automated and may be inaccurate, incomplete, misleading, or wrong. It is provided for informational purposes only. You should independently verify it and should not rely on it as medical, nutritional, financial, or other professional advice.

We do not send your food log, your goals, your spending history, your allergies, or your account identity to an artificial intelligence provider in order to receive advice about you.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

  • cloud infrastructure and authentication providers that host the Service, including Amazon Web Services
  • artificial intelligence providers, currently OpenAI, for the limited processing described in Section 4
  • analytics providers that help us understand how the Service is used, currently PostHog, subject to the limits described in Section 2.7
  • payment providers, where a paid subscription is offered, including Apple and Google
  • professional advisors, and any successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy
  • and legal authorities, where required by law or legal process, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.

We do not sell user personal data.

Certain reference data used by the Service, such as published nutrition figures and food pantry addresses, is shared across all users. That data describes a food or a ZIP code rather than a person and contains no user information of any kind.

We may use anonymized and aggregated information, such as a typical price range for a product in an area, to operate and improve the Service. Aggregated information is not linked to you and cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information and the entries you create for as long as your account is active, so that your history remains available to you. We retain information only as long as necessary to operate the Service, to comply with legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements.

Photographs are discarded immediately after processing and are not retained. Diagnostic logs are retained for a limited period for troubleshooting.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections, access controls limited to what each part of the Service needs, monitoring, and logging. Authentication is handled by a managed identity provider, and credentials are stored on your device in the secure storage that your operating system provides.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and notify us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

8. Your Rights

You may review and update your profile information, your goals, your budgets, and your entries at any time in the app, and you may correct or delete any individual entry. You may change your password, control your reminder preference, and request deletion of your account.

Depending on where you live, additional rights may apply under local privacy laws, including rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@mealsnmoney.com from your registered email address.

9. Age Requirement

The Service is intended only for individuals 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a person under 18, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed and stored in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service, and where required we will ask you to review and accept the updated policy.

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your MealsNMoney account directly in the app. Open the account menu, select My profile, choose Delete account, read the confirmation message, and confirm to permanently delete your account.

If you are unable to access your account, you may request deletion by emailing support@mealsnmoney.com from your registered email address. We permanently remove your account profile and the associated user data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal, security, or compliance obligations.
