4. Estimates, Accuracy, and Your Responsibility to Verify

Calorie counts, nutrient figures, portion sizes, prices, price ranges, and estimated totals presented in the Service are estimates. They may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong.

Nutrition figures are drawn primarily from public reference data, including the United States Department of Agriculture FoodData Central database, and from product information available to us. Reference data describes a generic or representative food, not the specific item you ate. Preparation, portion size, brand, and recipe all change the real values.

Where a figure is unknown, the Service is designed to show it as unknown rather than as zero. An absent value means that no measurement was available to us. It does not mean that the food contains none of that nutrient.

Prices are shown as ranges and vary by store, by location, and over time. The Service does not guarantee that any item is available at any store, or at any price.

You are responsible for reviewing and correcting any figure before relying on it. Estimates in the Service are editable for exactly that reason. You should not rely on the Service for any purpose where an inaccurate figure could cause harm.

5. Food Allergies, Intolerances, and Food Safety

Do not rely on MealsNMoney to keep you safe from a food allergy.

The Service allows you to record food allergies so that matching items can be filtered out of suggestions and flagged in your log where we hold allergen information for the food. The filter is automated. It compares the allergens you have recorded against the allergen fields we hold for an item in our own food catalogue. It is a structured match against recorded data, run twice. It is not an assessment of whether a food is safe for you, and no person reviews it. It operates only on the allergen and ingredient information available to us for a given item. That information may be missing, incomplete, out of date, or wrong.

The filter operates only on items the Service puts in front of you from its own catalogue. Anything you type in yourself is not checked at all. We hold no ingredient or allergen information for text you type, and we do not compare it against the allergies you have recorded. A food you search for, and a food you record as having eaten, is matched only where we hold allergen information for it, and the absence of a flag is not a statement that we checked.

The Service cannot detect cross-contact or cross-contamination, cannot account for a change a manufacturer makes to a formulation or to a facility, cannot know how an item was prepared, stored, or served, and cannot evaluate an item for which no allergen information exists. An item that is not flagged has not been shown to be safe. It has only not been matched.

No grocery list, meal suggestion, or log entry produced by the Service is, or is represented to be, allergen free, free from cross-contact, or safe for any particular person.

You must read the packaging and the ingredient label on every product before you buy or consume it, and you must confirm preparation with the establishment for any food you did not prepare yourself. If you have a severe, anaphylactic, or otherwise life-threatening allergy or intolerance, follow the guidance of your physician or allergist and treat the Service as no part of that plan.
